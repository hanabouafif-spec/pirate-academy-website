Why the Compass uses these five areas

Connection

Open, calm discussion and early disclosure.

Coaching

Critical thinking and safe digital judgement.

Structure

Open supervision

Open, proportionate and understood oversight.

Growing independence

A visible path from support toward freedom.

Why these five areas?

The five areas are a Pirate Academy synthesis for parent reflection. They are not presented as a validated five-factor psychological scale. The structure brings together recurring themes from parental-mediation, family-media and adolescent-development guidance:

  • Connection reflects caregiver–child relationships, conversation and children’s willingness to seek help or disclose online problems.
  • Coaching reflects active or enabling mediation, shared use, explanation and development of digital skills.
  • Structure reflects clear family expectations, routines and proportionate boundaries.
  • Open supervision reflects monitoring and safety settings that are visible, explained and adjusted to context.
  • Growing independence reflects age, maturity, self-regulation and a gradual path toward autonomy.

References used to form the five-area synthesis

  • Netsafe New Zealand, Digital parenting strategies and behaviours of New Zealand parents.
  • Livingstone and colleagues, research on enabling and restrictive parental mediation and children’s digital skills.
  • American Academy of Pediatrics, The Family Media Plan (2024) and Digital Ecosystems, Children, and Adolescents (2026).
  • American Psychological Association, Health Advisory on Social Media Use in Adolescence (2023).
  • Banić and Orehovački, systematic review of parenting strategies in a digital environment (2024/2025 publication record).
  • Helsper, Veltri and Livingstone, research on risk perception, digital skills and parental mediation (2024 article; 2025 journal volume).
  • Hernandez and colleagues, study of restrictive, active and deference monitoring in early adolescence (2023; corrected publication 2025).
  • UNICEF, 2026 briefs on children’s AI use and AI companions; Common Sense Media, 2025–2026 AI-companion research and safeguards.

Research-informed educational reflection only. The Compass is not a clinical assessment, diagnosis or substitute for safeguarding, medical or mental-health support. Evidence and examples should be reviewed on a visible version cycle because digital products and research change quickly.
